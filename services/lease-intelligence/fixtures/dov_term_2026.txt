DEED OF VARIATION OF LEASE


LANDLORD:        KITCHENER HOLDINGS LIMITED

TENANT:          MARLOW & FINCH ARCHITECTS LIMITED

PREMISES:        Level 3, 14 Kitchener Street, Auckland Central 1010

HEAD LEASE:      Deed of Lease dated 18 March 2024, Reference LS-2024-014


THIS DEED is made on the 9th day of January 2026


BACKGROUND

A.   By a Deed of Lease dated 18 March 2024 ("the Lease"), the Landlord
     leased the Premises to the Tenant for a term expiring 31 March 2030.

B.   The parties have agreed to extend the term of the Lease.


OPERATIVE PART

1.   VARIATION DATE

1.1  This Deed takes effect on and from 1 April 2026.

1.2  Except as varied by this Deed, the Lease continues in full force and
     effect.


2.   VARIATION OF TERM

2.1  Clause 2.1 of the Lease is deleted and replaced with the following:

     "The Landlord leases the Premises to the Tenant for a term of eleven (11)
     years commencing on 1 April 2024 and expiring on 31 March 2035, unless
     sooner determined in accordance with this Deed."

2.2  For the avoidance of doubt, the rent payable under clause 3.1 of the
     Lease is not varied by this Deed.


EXECUTED AS A DEED
