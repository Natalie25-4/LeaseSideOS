DEED OF VARIATION OF LEASE


LANDLORD:        KITCHENER HOLDINGS LIMITED

TENANT:          MARLOW & FINCH ARCHITECTS LIMITED

PREMISES:        Level 3, 14 Kitchener Street, Auckland Central 1010

HEAD LEASE:      Deed of Lease dated 18 March 2024, Reference LS-2024-014


THIS DEED is made on the 14th day of February 2026


BACKGROUND

A.   By a Deed of Lease dated 18 March 2024 ("the Lease"), the Landlord
     leased the Premises to the Tenant.

B.   The parties have agreed to vary the rent payable under the Lease.


OPERATIVE PART

1.   VARIATION DATE

1.1  This Deed takes effect on and from 1 April 2026.


2.   VARIATION OF RENT

2.1  Clause 3.1 of the Lease is deleted and replaced with the following:

     "The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord the annual rent of ONE HUNDRED AND
     FIFTY FIVE THOUSAND DOLLARS ($155,000.00) plus GST, by equal monthly
     instalments of TWELVE THOUSAND NINE HUNDRED AND SIXTEEN DOLLARS AND
     SIXTY SIX CENTS ($12,916.66) plus GST, payable in advance on the first
     day of each month."


EXECUTED AS A DEED
