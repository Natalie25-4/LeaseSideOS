DEED OF LEASE


LANDLORD:        KITCHENER HOLDINGS LIMITED

TENANT:          MARLOW & FINCH ARCHITECTS LIMITED

PREMISES:        Level 3, 14 Kitchener Street, Auckland Central 1010


                                                    Reference: LS-2024-014


THIS DEED is made on the 18th day of March 2024


BACKGROUND

A.   The Landlord is the registered proprietor of the land and buildings
     situated at 14 Kitchener Street, Auckland Central.

B.   The Landlord has agreed to lease to the Tenant, and the Tenant has
     agreed to take on lease, the Premises on the terms set out in this Deed.


1.   DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1  In this Deed, unless the context otherwise requires:

     "Building" means the building erected on the Land known as Kitchener
     Chambers, together with all improvements from time to time.

     "Commencement Date" means 1 April 2024.

     "Outgoings" means the expenses described in clause 5 of this Deed.

     "Premises" means Level 3 of the Building comprising approximately
     412 square metres of net lettable area, together with the car parks
     described in the First Schedule.

     "Rent" means the rent payable under clause 3 of this Deed, as reviewed
     from time to time in accordance with clause 4.

1.2  Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

1.3  References to a party include that party's executors, administrators,
     successors and permitted assigns.
2.   TERM

2.1  The Landlord leases the Premises to the Tenant for a term of six (6)
     years commencing on 1 April 2024 and expiring on 31 March 2030, unless
     sooner determined in accordance with this Deed.

2.2  The Tenant shall have two (2) rights of renewal of three (3) years each,
     exercisable by written notice given to the Landlord not less than three
     (3) months and not more than six (6) months before the expiry of the
     then current term.

2.3  If the Tenant exercises a right of renewal, the renewed term shall be on
     the same terms and conditions as this Deed, except that the rent shall be
     determined in accordance with clause 4.3 and the number of further rights
     of renewal shall be reduced accordingly.

2.4  The final expiry date, assuming both rights of renewal are exercised,
     is 31 March 2036.


3.   RENT

3.1  The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord the annual rent of ONE HUNDRED AND
     FORTY FIVE THOUSAND DOLLARS ($145,000.00) plus GST, by equal monthly
     instalments of TWELVE THOUSAND AND EIGHTY THREE DOLLARS AND THIRTY THREE
     CENTS ($12,083.33) plus GST, payable in advance on the first day of each
     month.

3.2  The first instalment shall be paid on the Commencement Date and shall be
     apportioned if the Commencement Date is not the first day of a month.

3.3  All payments shall be made by direct credit to a bank account nominated
     by the Landlord, without deduction or set-off of any kind.

3.4  If any Rent remains unpaid for fourteen (14) days after the due date,
     the Tenant shall pay interest on the overdue amount at the rate of
     twelve percent (12%) per annum, calculated daily from the due date until
     payment is received.
4.   RENT REVIEW

4.1  The Rent shall be reviewed on each second anniversary of the Commencement
     Date, being 1 April 2026 and 1 April 2028 (each a "Review Date").

4.2  At each Review Date the Rent shall be increased by the percentage increase
     in the Consumers Price Index (All Groups) for the twelve month period
     ending on the quarter immediately preceding the Review Date, provided that
     the Rent shall not decrease and shall not increase by more than five
     percent (5%) in any one review.

4.3  On the commencement of any renewed term the Rent shall be reviewed to the
     current market rent for the Premises. If the parties cannot agree the
     market rent within thirty (30) days, the matter shall be referred to a
     registered valuer agreed between the parties or, failing agreement,
     appointed by the President of the New Zealand Institute of Valuers.

4.4  Until a reviewed Rent is determined, the Tenant shall continue to pay the
     Rent payable immediately before the Review Date, and any shortfall shall
     be paid within fourteen (14) days of determination.


5.   OUTGOINGS

5.1  In addition to the Rent, the Tenant shall pay the Tenant's proportion of
     all Outgoings in respect of the Building, being 23.4% based on the ratio
     of the net lettable area of the Premises to the net lettable area of the
     Building.

5.2  Outgoings include rates and levies, building insurance premiums, body
     corporate levies, cleaning and maintenance of common areas, management
     expenses, lift maintenance, fire protection servicing, and the cost of
     supplying water, gas and electricity to common areas.

5.3  Outgoings do not include the Landlord's income tax, the cost of structural
     repairs, capital improvements, or expenses attributable to vacant
     tenancies within the Building.

5.4  The Tenant shall pay Outgoings monthly in advance based on the Landlord's
     reasonable estimate, with a reconciliation within three (3) months of the
     end of each financial year.
6.   PERMITTED USE

6.1  The Tenant shall use the Premises solely as commercial offices for the
     practice of architecture and associated design services, and for no
     other purpose without the prior written consent of the Landlord, such
     consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

6.2  The Tenant shall not use the Premises for any noxious, offensive or
     hazardous purpose, nor permit anything that may invalidate any insurance
     policy held by the Landlord in respect of the Building.

6.3  The Tenant shall comply with all statutes, regulations and bylaws relating
     to its use of the Premises, including the Health and Safety at Work Act
     2015.


7.   MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR

7.1  The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises, including all fixtures,
     fittings, floor coverings, window coverings and internal glazing, in good
     order, condition and repair, fair wear and tear excepted.

7.2  The Landlord shall maintain and keep in good repair the structure, roof,
     exterior walls, foundations, common areas, lifts and building services of
     the Building.

7.3  The Tenant shall not make any alterations or additions to the Premises
     without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

7.4  The Tenant shall permit the Landlord and its agents to enter the Premises
     at reasonable times on reasonable notice to inspect their condition.


8.   INSURANCE

8.1  The Landlord shall insure the Building for its full replacement value
     against fire, flood, earthquake and other usual risks, and shall maintain
     twelve (12) months loss of rents cover.

8.2  The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than
     TWO MILLION DOLLARS ($2,000,000.00) for any one event, and shall insure
     its own fixtures, fittings, stock and plate glass.

8.3  The Tenant shall provide evidence of the insurances required under clause
     8.2 to the Landlord on request, and in any event annually.
9.   ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

9.1  The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, mortgage or otherwise part with
     possession of the Premises or any part of them without the prior written
     consent of the Landlord, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld
     where the proposed assignee is respectable, responsible and financially
     sound.

9.2  The Tenant shall pay the Landlord's reasonable legal and administrative
     costs in considering any request for consent under clause 9.1.

9.3  Notwithstanding any assignment, the Tenant shall remain liable for the
     performance of the Tenant's obligations under this Deed for the balance
     of the then current term.


10.  DEFAULT

10.1 The Landlord may cancel this Deed and re-enter the Premises if:

     (a)  any Rent remains unpaid for thirty (30) days after the due date; or

     (b)  the Tenant breaches any other obligation under this Deed and fails
          to remedy that breach within twenty (20) working days of written
          notice from the Landlord; or

     (c)  the Tenant becomes insolvent, is placed in liquidation, or has a
          receiver appointed over its assets.

10.2 Cancellation under clause 10.1 does not affect any right of the Landlord
     to recover damages for breach of this Deed.


11.  REINSTATEMENT ON EXPIRY

11.1 On the expiry or earlier determination of this Deed, the Tenant shall
     remove its fixtures, fittings and signage and make good any damage caused
     by that removal, and shall yield up the Premises in a clean and tidy
     condition consistent with the Tenant's obligations under clause 7.1.

11.2 Any of the Tenant's property remaining in the Premises fourteen (14) days
     after expiry may be disposed of by the Landlord, and the Tenant shall pay
     the reasonable costs of disposal.


12.  QUIET ENJOYMENT

12.1 The Tenant, paying the Rent and performing the Tenant's obligations under
     this Deed, may quietly hold and enjoy the Premises throughout the term
     without interruption by the Landlord.


FIRST SCHEDULE — CAR PARKS

Four (4) car parks located on Basement Level 1, numbered B1-14 to B1-17
inclusive, at a licence fee of SIXTY DOLLARS ($60.00) plus GST per park per
week, payable with the Rent.
