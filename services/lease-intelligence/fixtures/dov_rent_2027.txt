DEED OF VARIATION OF LEASE


LANDLORD:        KITCHENER HOLDINGS LIMITED

TENANT:          MARLOW & FINCH ARCHITECTS LIMITED

PREMISES:        Level 3, 14 Kitchener Street, Auckland Central 1010

HEAD LEASE:      Deed of Lease dated 18 March 2024, Reference LS-2024-014


THIS DEED is made on the 20th day of February 2027


BACKGROUND

A.   By a Deed of Lease dated 18 March 2024 ("the Lease"), the Landlord
     leased the Premises to the Tenant.

B.   The parties have agreed to vary the Lease in the manner set out below.


OPERATIVE PART

1.   VARIATION DATE

1.1  This Deed takes effect on and from 1 April 2027 ("the Variation Date").

1.2  Except as varied by this Deed, the Lease continues in full force and
     effect in accordance with its terms.


2.   VARIATION OF RENT

2.1  With effect from the Variation Date, clause 3.1 of the Lease is deleted
     and replaced with the following:

     "The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord the annual rent of ONE HUNDRED AND
     SIXTY THOUSAND DOLLARS ($160,000.00) plus GST, by equal monthly
     instalments of THIRTEEN THOUSAND THREE HUNDRED AND THIRTY THREE DOLLARS
     AND THIRTY THREE CENTS ($13,333.33) plus GST, payable in advance on the
     first day of each month."

2.2  The rent review provisions in clause 4 of the Lease are unaffected by
     this Deed and continue to apply.


3.   NO OTHER VARIATION

3.1  No provision of the Lease other than clause 3.1 is varied by this Deed.


EXECUTED AS A DEED
